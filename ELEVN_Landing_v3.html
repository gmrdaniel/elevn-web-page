What exactly is ELEVN?
ELEVN is a creator ecosystem where you'll find real brand opportunities, paid programs, courses, live events, and a community of creators from different verticals and regions. Apply once and get access to all of it.
Do I need a huge following to join?
No. ELEVN is built specifically for emerging and mid-sized creators. What matters is the quality of your content and your engagement. Not just the number on your profile.
Is it free?
Yes, joining ELEVN is completely free. You only go through a quick profile validation so we can match you with the right opportunities.
What kind of brands will I work with?
We partner with global brands like Dove, L'Oréal, Procter & Gamble, and many others, as well as platforms like Meta, Pinterest, YouTube, and Tubi. Every brand on ELEVN has been vetted by our team.
Do I keep ownership of my content?
Always. Every collaboration on ELEVN has clear terms that protect your rights. You own your content, your brand, and your audience.
